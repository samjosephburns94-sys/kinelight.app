# Changelog

Kinelight for Mac. Downloads are on the [releases page](https://github.com/samjosephburns94-sys/kinelight.app/releases);
subscribers also get them from their Subscriptions page on [kinelight.brnzy.co.uk](https://kinelight.brnzy.co.uk).

## 0.2.0 — 5 November 2026

### New
- **Demo mode.** A free demo is the Control page on the festival demo stage: fire looks, ride the faders, press GO
  and watch it in 3D. Everything else — patching, the stage, the library, groups, looks, settings — shows what's in
  each plan, and nothing is sent to the lights. A banner shows the days left and opens the plans.
- Demos come from the website: **Try the free demo** at kinelight.brnzy.co.uk/demo (one per account), or from the
  Kinelight team as a demo code. They end by themselves.
- **Subscribe and the same Mac unlocks** within half a minute — the demo stage stays as it is.
- Account tab: a demo shows what it includes, the days left and See the plans.

### Changed
- Light output stays on for a Mac that was already sending on a paid plan, even if its licence changes mid-session —
  the lights are never cut in the middle of a show.

## 0.1.0 — 5 November 2026

First release.

### Show control
- **Control**: group tiles, the stage plan (or 3D, or a camera), faders with flash, GO and Back, the cue stack, looks,
  smoke & FX and a big Blackout.
- **Busk**: a page of windows you choose — groups, colour, tempo, layered effects, looks, positions and beam — with
  masters and a busk fader.
- **Shows**: acts and pieces with their songs, back-wall video and rehearsal video; cues on the music, a pyro lane,
  live REC, the cue sheet and a pre-show check.
- **Looks & gobos**: describe a look in your own words; washes take the colour, spots take gobos and movement.
- **Pyro & SFX**: arm, then fire — switch packs, flame, CO₂, cold spark, confetti; haze and smoke on a level.
- **Fixtures**: 670 profiles with exact channel orders, quick patch by typing, Open Fixture Library, Avolites and JSON
  import, a venue's fixture list from a PDF, and your own profiles.
- **Art-Net & sACN**: many nodes, Find nodes, a live network monitor and OSC in and out.
- **iPad & phone remote**, **second screens** and the **video wall** on an extra display.
- **Controllers**: APC mini mk2 out of the box, MIDI Learn for any controller, Stream Deck via shortcuts or OSC.
- **Camera tracking**: follow dancers from a camera or a rehearsal video.

### The stage in 3D
- **Festival demo stage** on first launch (and Settings → Load demo rig): a 20 × 14 m outdoor main stage with a
  16 × 7.5 m LED wall, 104 lights on six trusses, pyro, groups and nine ready-made looks.
- **Outdoor stages**: night sky, a field, the stage roof, PA, a crowd and front of house (Stage → Outdoor stage).
- The LED wall's **Visuals**: built-in moving pictures that follow the rig's colour and the beat.
- Pyro and effects are drawn as they fire: CO₂ plumes, flames, cold sparks and confetti.

### Account
- **Sign in from the app**, choose main or backup Mac, and swap them live from the app or the website.
- **Plans**: Kinelight (control and patching) and Kinelight Pro (everything); screens outside your plan show what they
  are and Upgrade. Plan changes reach the Mac within half a minute.
- **Account tab**: your plan, your Macs, what's switched on, and ideas or problems sent straight to the team.
- Works offline: the licence checks in at least every 15 days.
